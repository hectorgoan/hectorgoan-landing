Why Lumina isn’t on the Mac App Store

Every app on the Mac App Store runs inside Apple’s App Sandbox, a walled-off space that keeps it away from the rest of your Mac. For most apps that’s the right call. But Lumina’s whole job is the rest of your Mac, and inside the sandbox most of its utilities couldn’t work:

  • Other apps’ files

    Uninstaller and Cleanup find and remove what other apps leave in your Library. A sandboxed app can’t see other apps’ files.

  • Asking other apps to act

    Quit All, Empty Trash, Appearance and Uninstaller ask Finder and System Events to act for you. The sandbox doesn’t let an app control other apps this way.

  • Keyboard and pointer

    Stay Green and Keyboard Lock work with your keyboard and pointer, through the Accessibility access the sandbox restricts.

  • Private hardware readings

    System, Media and Batteries read your Mac’s temperature and fan speed, what’s playing in any app, and your AirPods’ and accessories’ charge. macOS has no public interface for these, and App Store apps may use only public ones.

Sold directly, signed every time.

A sandboxed Lumina would be a handful of toggles. So Lumina is sold directly instead, as many of the best Mac utilities are: you download it from our site, pay once, and it updates itself in place. Every update is cryptographically signed, and Lumina checks that signature before installing anything.

Only what you ask.

Lumina only touches what you ask it to. It never removes system files, every uninstall goes to the Trash first so you can put it back, and nothing about your Mac ever leaves it.

Install in a minute.

Lumina comes from this site, not the App Store, so macOS asks you once to allow it.

  1. 1 Download and unzip Get Lumina.zip and open it.
  2. 2 Move it to Applications Drag Lumina into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. 3 Click Open Anyway The first time, macOS says it can’t check Lumina for malicious software. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the message about Lumina. System Settings › Privacy & Security

You only do this once. Some utilities ask for a permission the first time you use them, like Accessibility for Keyboard Lock. Lumina checks for updates when it opens and every six hours, and installs them in place.
