Privacy Policy

Lumina keeps your data on your Mac.

Last updated November 4, 2026

The short version

Lumina has no account and doesn’t upload your files, your activity or anything you see in its panel. Everything its utilities read, like storage, processes, batteries and media, stays on your Mac.

Anonymous usage count

Once a day, Lumina sends a small anonymous report so I know how many people use it and which versions are in use:

  • a random ID made on your Mac, not linked to you;
  • whether Lumina is on its trial or licensed, and a one-way fingerprint of your licence key (not the key itself);
  • Lumina’s version and your macOS version.

It’s stored on Cloudflare and used only to count installs. You can turn it off in Lumina’s Settings › Share Anonymous Usage.

Updates

To check for updates, Lumina downloads a list of versions from GitHub. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address when this happens (see GitHub’s privacy statement).

Buying a licence

Payments are handled by Paddle, the Merchant of Record for Lumina. Paddle collects your payment details under its privacy policy; I never see your card. I receive your name, email address and country so I can send your licence key and help you with your order. I don’t sell or share them, and I don’t send marketing email.

Your licence key

Lumina checks your key on your Mac, offline. It isn’t sent anywhere, except as the one-way fingerprint above.

This website

This site has no ads, analytics or tracking cookies. It remembers your light or dark choice in your browser, and loads its font from Google Fonts.

Your rights

You can ask me what data I hold about you, and to correct or delete it, at hectorgoan@gmail.com.
